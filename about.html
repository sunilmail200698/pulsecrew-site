Our Story

We started with a simple observation.

PulseCrew started with a simple observation: when an ICU bed opens or an OT slot needs covering, hospitals don't have hours to spend on phone calls and paperwork — and qualified nurses shouldn't have to wait days to find their next shift. We built a real-time matching layer that connects verified specialists with hospitals the moment a gap opens, so neither side is left waiting.

Traditional staffing agencies weren't built for this kind of urgency. Manual outreach, opaque commission structures, and credential checks that happen after a nurse is already on the floor all add friction at exactly the moment hospitals can least afford it. PulseCrew exists to remove that friction.

Our Mission

To make sure no critical-care shift goes uncovered and no qualified nurse goes unseen, by matching verified ICU, OT and ER talent with hospitals in real time, without ever trading safety for speed.

Our Values

What we hold ourselves to

These shape how the platform is built and how we treat hospitals and nurses.

Urgency, with care

Critical care can't wait. We design every step to close gaps in minutes, not days, and never at the expense of a check that protects patients.

Verification before everything

No nurse appears in a hospital's search results until their registration, specialty competency and background checks clear.

Radical transparency

One flat per-shift fee for hospitals. Nurses see their full rate before they accept. No commission layers, no surprise line items.

Respect for nurses

Experienced nurses deserve flexibility, clear pay and a say in their schedule. Our workflows were shaped by nurses who've lived the alternative.

Privacy and security by default

Credential data, shift records and payment details are protected, and shared with a hospital only as far as needed to confirm a match.

Accountability

Automation does the matching, and people stand behind it. A live coordination desk backs up time-sensitive shifts.
