About PulseCrew

Healthcare staffing built for the moments that can't wait.

PulseCrew started with a simple observation: when an ICU bed opens or an OT slot needs covering, hospitals don't have hours to spend on phone calls and paperwork — and qualified nurses shouldn't have to wait days to find their next shift. What We Believe

Why PulseCrew exists

Traditional staffing agencies weren't built for this kind of urgency. Manual outreach, opaque commission structures, and credential checks that happen after a nurse is already on the floor all add friction at exactly the moment hospitals can least afford it. PulseCrew is the layer that should have existed from the start — verification before a shift is ever offered, pricing that's visible upfront, and the right nurse surfaced automatically instead of found by chance.

Speed, by design

Every part of the matching flow — from posting a shift to confirming a nurse — is built to close gaps in minutes, not days.

Verification first

No nurse appears in a hospital's search results until their GNC registration, specialty competency, and background checks clear.

No hidden markups

Hospitals see one flat per-shift fee upfront. Nurses see their full rate before they accept — no agency commissions buried in the bill.

Built with working nurses

Every workflow — from shift alerts to payment timing — was shaped by feedback from ICU, OT, and ER nurses who've lived the alternative.

Security & privacy by default

Credential data, shift records, and payment details are encrypted end-to-end and shared with a hospital only to the extent needed to confirm a match.

Support when it matters

A live coordination desk backs up the platform for time-sensitive shifts — automated matching with a human layer behind it, not instead of it.
