FAQ

Common questions from hospitals and nurses

How quickly can a shift be covered?

Most ICU, OT, and ER shifts are matched with a verified nurse in under 30 minutes of posting. Highly specialized or last-minute requests may take longer, but every request stays active until it's filled.

How are nurse credentials verified?

Every nurse completes GNC registration verification, a specialty-specific clinical competency assessment, and background and reference checks before they're eligible to accept shifts. Compliance is monitored continuously, not just at onboarding.

What is the pricing structure for hospitals?

PulseCrew charges one flat per-shift fee, disclosed before you confirm a request. There are no commission markups, no surge pricing, and no hidden line items on your invoice.

How do nurses receive shift payments?

Nurses see the guaranteed pay for a shift before accepting it and receive payouts on a fixed weekly cycle directly through the app — no chasing agencies for payment.

Privacy & Data Use

Your information stays with us.

We collect only what's needed to verify and contact you: hospital contact details for shift requests, and name, contact, nursing council registration, and credential details for nurse applications. Responses are stored securely within our Google Workspace and are accessible only to the PulseCrew team. To review, update, or request removal of your data, email support@pulsecrew.health.
